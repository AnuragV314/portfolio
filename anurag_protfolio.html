About

I'm a Founding Software Engineer working at the intersection of backend systems and applied machine learning. I care less about which framework is trending and more about the reasoning underneath it — why a query plan is fast, why a model generalizes, why an architecture holds up once real traffic hits it.

A mathematics background shapes how I approach engineering: as a series of testable claims rather than guesses. I like being early — setting up the systems, conventions, and infrastructure that a product's future engineering team will build on top of.
